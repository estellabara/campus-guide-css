Food Guide

Eating on Campus

Eating on campus can be a challenge, but there are plenty of options available. Here are tips to help you navigate the dining options:

  1. Check out the campus dining halls for a variety of meal options.
  2. Look for local restaurants and cafes that offer student discounts.
  3. Consider meal prepping to save time and money.

Healthy Eating Tips

Maintaining a healthy diet is important for your overall well-being. Here are some tips to help you eat healthily while on campus:

  • Incorporate fruits and vegetables into your meals.
  • Choose whole grains over refined grains.
  • Limit your intake of sugary drinks and snacks.
